Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Overview

FitnessCalc ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Do Not Collect

All calculator inputs — including weight, height, age, body measurements, and fitness data — are processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. This data is never sent to our servers, never stored, and never shared with third parties.

Information We May Collect

Like most websites, we may collect standard server log information including:

  • IP address (anonymized)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring URL
  • Date and time of access

This data is used in aggregate form only to understand how the site is used and to improve our calculators. It is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Cookies

FitnessCalc may use cookies for basic site functionality, such as remembering your unit preferences (imperial vs. metric). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that identify individual users.

Third-Party Services

We may use Google Fonts, which loads font files from Google's servers. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle font request data. We use no other third-party tracking or analytics services.

Advertising

FitnessCalc may display advertisements to support free access to our tools. If ad networks are used, they may set their own cookies in accordance with their privacy policies. We do not allow ad networks to access any health data you enter into our calculators.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal health data, there is nothing for us to provide, modify, or delete relating to calculator usage. For any other data requests related to server logs, please contact us directly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of FitnessCalc after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through our website.
